Welcome

Your ownlodgeabove theopen sea.

Pinnacle Point Estate is our own home on the cliffs west of Mossel Bay, and over the summer holidays it can be yours. Take your coffee out to the sea air, walk the cliff paths through the fynbos, play a round on one of the most dramatic golf courses in the country, then braai as the sun goes down over the water.

On your doorstep

Golf, caves and whales from the deck.

  • On the estate

    Pinnacle Point Golf Club

    Eighteen holes along the cliff edge, with carries over ravines and the ocean on almost every hole.

  • Below the estate

    The Pinnacle Point caves

    Some of the world’s most important archaeological sites for early modern humans. Book a guided tour with Point of Human Origins.

  • Walking distance

    Cliff paths & fynbos

    Walk the estate paths through coastal fynbos, with views over the sea and the bay.

  • June – November

    Whale watching

    Southern right and humpback whales pass close to shore in season. Keep an eye on the water from the deck.
